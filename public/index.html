Apple will not let me make a widget on my own, so we are borrowing a free app called Scriptable. It is a bit of a faff, but only once, and I have already done the hard parts.

  1. 1 Get Scriptable from the App Store if you do not have it. Open the App Store
  2. 2 Tap the button below. It copies me and opens Scriptable on an empty script.
  3. 3 Press and hold on the empty script, tap Paste, then Done. Call it Leave, Bye.
  4. 4 Hold down your Home Screen, tap the +, find Scriptable, pick the medium one.
  5. 5 Hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, set Script to Leave, Bye and Parameter to your name.
