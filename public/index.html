A panda on your Home Screen

Apple only lets real App Store apps make widgets, so this one borrows a free app called Scriptable. Annoying, but it is a one-time thing and I have done the fiddly parts.

  1. 1 Install Scriptable from the App Store, if you have not. Open the App Store
  2. 2 Tap the button below. It copies the widget and opens Scriptable on a blank script.
  3. 3 Press and hold in the empty script, tap Paste, then Done. Name it Leave, Bye.
  4. 4 Hold the Home Screen, tap +, pick Scriptable, add a medium widget.
  5. 5 Hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, set Script to Leave, Bye and Parameter to your name.
