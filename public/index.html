How to install it

  1. 1 Tap the Share button at the bottom. The little square with the arrow pointing up.
  2. 2 Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3 Tap Add. Then open the app from your Home Screen, not from Safari.

Notifications only work when you open it from the Home Screen. That's Apple's rule, not mine.

Settings

Home Screen widget

Apple doesn't let an app like this add its own widget, so it needs a free app called Scriptable. It's fiddly.

Easiest option: ask Emmanuel to set it up for you.

Or do it yourself:

  1. 1 Install Scriptable from the App Store. Open the App Store
  2. 2 Tap the button below. It copies the widget and opens Scriptable.
  3. 3 Press and hold in the empty script, tap Paste, then Done. Name it Leave, Bye.
  4. 4 Hold your Home Screen, tap +, choose Scriptable, add the medium widget.
  5. 5 Hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, set Script to Leave, Bye and Parameter to your name.
